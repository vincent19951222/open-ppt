# MR Red Tech · healthcare STYLE DESIGN SYSTEM

## PART A — Category Baseline (Medical-Technology Product Solution Minimum)

A serious, restrained product-solution deck for a medical-technology company (devices / IVD / healthcare IT), benchmarked against enterprise solution proposals delivered to hospital procurement committees, clinical departments, and regulatory-aware executives: every page is auditable, every claim traceable, zero decorative noise.

Content and layout:
1. Solution narrative: clinical/operational pain point → solution overview (one-page product architecture) → module-by-module breakdown → workflow/process integration → performance data and validation evidence → compliance and quality system → deployment, service, and business value → summary and next steps.
2. High information density: multi-module body pages built from flow diagrams, step arrows, comparison tables, and metric panels — never "one large sentence + expansive blank space".
3. Process is the signature content type: clinical pathways, sample/data flows, and deployment steps are drawn as explicit step-arrow flows or fine-line flowcharts, not described in prose paragraphs.

Visual style:
1. Aesthetic: clinical, precise, corporate; the deck must read as produced by an engineering-driven medical company, not a marketing agency.
2. Typography: a single hei-style sans family throughout (中文黑体); hierarchy built on weight and size; numbers column-aligned.
3. Color: one brand red family plus black, white, and neutral grays; every hue on a page must map to a meaning (brand/emphasis, neutral structure, risk).
4. Graphics: fine-line flowcharts, square-cornered modules, flat charts with direct data labels; no 3D, no pictographs as data.

Structure and discipline:
1. Every body-page title is a complete assertion (a conclusion sentence), stating the point the evidence below proves.
2. One named structure per page: a step-arrow flow, an architecture layer diagram, a comparison table, a KPI panel, a screenshot walkthrough, a timeline — chosen by the content, never a loose collage.
3. Clinical and performance data must carry source, sample scope, and time period; fabricated clinical evidence, certifications, or customer cases are strictly forbidden — mark missing material explicitly as placeholder/to-be-supplied.
4. Comparison pages (vs. legacy method, vs. competitor class) must compare on identical dimensions, row by row.
5. Most pages close with a one-line bottom takeaway converting the evidence into a single conclusion.
6. Fixed footer discipline: source/footnote line at bottom left, page number at bottom right; never omit them. Generated decks never add watermarks or confidentiality stamps unless the user provides them (the brand logo rule in PART B is the sanctioned exception).
7. Numbers must carry units, ranges, and definitions; uncertainty is expressed through monitorable metrics, not hedging language.

Default baseline (PART B may override): body pages are high-density and multi-module, carrying at least one flow diagram, comparison table, screenshot walkthrough, or KPI panel; build framework and architecture diagrams with fine, precise lines; keep dominant hues on one page within the brand palette only.

**Read-the-titles test**: reading only the page titles across the deck should let you hear the complete solution story; if they do not read coherently, the structure is not working.

> Priority note: this section is the minimum baseline for medical-technology solution contexts. When the Signature System below (PART B) conflicts with it, follow the Signature System.

### Global Prohibitions (AI Default Patterns · Mandatory)

1. **Cards as sanctioned containers — square corners only**: this style system explicitly uses square-cornered card modules (PART B); rounded rectangles, pill shapes, and any radius > 0 are prohibited everywhere.
2. **No unmotivated AI color schemes**: what is banned is the mediocre, unjustified default — blue-purple gradients, cyan-purple neon, rainbow flares, glass cards, glowing borders; no hue outside the brand palette may appear.
3. **No decorative elements**: no decorative photography, illustrations, cartoon imagery, emoji, or texture overlays; icons only as single-color line drawings serving a category or step. Product photos and software UI screenshots are evidence content, not decoration — see PART B.
4. **Transparent chart backgrounds**: transparent or the page background color.
5. **No empty regions**: leave no solid block of blank space in the body content area; every planned region must be filled with real content.

## PART B — Signature System (takes precedence over PART A in conflicts)

One-line style signature: Mindray-style brand deep red governs titles, ❯ arrow markers, and key figures; white and light-gray grounds carry dense red-black flow diagrams, state-coded step arrows, and square-cornered card modules; software UI screenshots pair with right-side explanation columns as a first-class layout; the cover is a full-bleed deep-red field with an oversized white bold title; data pages speak through oversized red numerals; every page carries the fixed bottom-right brand logo lockup, and precision-instrument detailing (hairlines, two-tier neutrals, spacing rhythm) supplies the premium finish — restrained, serious, high-density.

【Color Palette】
- Backgrounds: pure white #FFFFFF as the primary body background; light gray #F5F5F5 for zone panels, sidebars, table row banding, and bottom takeaway bars (sectioning only, never the full body background); cover and closing page use full-bleed brand red.
- Brand red family: deep red #C8102E is the primary brand color — page titles, ❯ arrow markers, key figures, active step arrows, primary chart series, table header rows, the fixed logo; deep maroon #8F0C20 for depth on the cover (lower band, subtle layering) and for negative/risk states in charts; light red tint #FBE9EB for emphasis fills inside tables and cards, used sparingly.
- Text: near-black #1A1A1A for body and headings on light grounds; #595959 for secondary explanation; #8C8C8C for footnotes, sources, and page numbers; white #FFFFFF for reversed type on red.
- Lines and inactive states: #D9D9D9 fine rules for grids, table borders, and module separation; #1A1A1A for framework skeleton lines when extra precision is needed; #BFBFBF for inactive/upcoming step arrows, inactive tabs, and unselected states — state coding is strictly red = active/current, gray = inactive/upcoming.
- Charts: series ordered brand red #C8102E → deep maroon #8F0C20 → near-black #1A1A1A → neutral grays #BFBFBF / #D9D9D9; at most 3 series colors per chart; negative/risk always deep maroon with a direct annotation — never green, blue, orange, or any hue outside this palette.
- Area discipline: on body pages, red tones cover at most ~20% of the page area (titles, markers, figures, key series); the cover is the only page where red dominates.

【Layout Skeleton】Source-canvas facts: 16:9, 12192000×6858000 EMU (approximately 1467×825 px). Every body page carries a fixed header: a small red ❯ marker followed by the bold red assertion title, aligned top-left, occupying roughly the top 10–12% of the page; a thin #D9D9D9 rule separates header from body. The body region is white with light-gray zone panels for explanation columns or grouped modules; flow and architecture diagrams sit in the main region, drawn in red-black fine lines. Screenshot pages place the software UI screenshot on the left 55–60% with the explanation column on the right. Fixed footer: source/footnote line bottom-left; the bottom-right corner of EVERY body page carries the bundled brand logo `assets/mindray-lockup-wide.png` (copy it into the deck's `media/` directory and reference it there), sized roughly 90–110pt wide, with the page number immediately to its left; never redraw, recolor, or substitute a text placeholder for the logo, and never cover it with other elements. Cover only: full-bleed deep red #C8102E field, oversized white bold title (left-aligned or left-of-center), one thin white horizontal rule, optional small white subtitle/date block bottom-left; no photography, no imagery on the cover; the red logo is omitted on full-red pages because it would be invisible — a white knockout version may be used only if the user supplies one. Chapter dividers: white ground, oversized light-gray chapter number, red ❯ marker, black bold chapter title; a vertical deep-red band on the left quarter is the acceptable alternate.

【Typography】Use a hei-style sans family throughout (中文黑体; render with {latin: Arial, ea: MiSans}; Source Han Sans / 思源黑体 is an acceptable substitute). Page title 24–28pt bold brand red, preceded by the red ❯ marker; section headings 16–20pt bold near-black; body 12–14pt; oversized KPI numerals 48–72pt bold brand red (e.g. 37.3%) with a 1-line 11–12pt caption beneath; footnotes 8–9pt gray. Titles use bold assertion sentences; no serif type, no italics except quotations, no all-caps paragraphs.

【Chart Language】Actually used: bar/column comparisons (brand red primary series vs. gray benchmark), line trends (solid red actual, gray dashed plan/target), state-coded step-arrow flows and fine-line flowcharts for clinical pathways and data flows, architecture layer diagrams, comparison tables, KPI panels.
- Bars/columns: brand red for the subject product/metric, #BFBFBF gray for baselines and competitor classes; write values directly on marks with white labels on red bars (dark labels on brand red fail contrast) and dark labels on gray bars; never rely on a legend alone.
- Lines: solid red for actuals, gray dashed for plans/forecasts labeled as such; mark inflection points with numbered dots and short explanations.
- Flows/steps: chevron or right-arrow sequences; completed/current steps in brand red with white bold labels, inactive/upcoming steps in #BFBFBF gray with #595959 dark-gray labels (white text on light gray fails contrast), with near-black step labels; decision nodes as square-corner diamonds; 4–8 nodes per flow; keep every arrow labeled or self-evident.
- Tables: brand-red header row with white bold text, white body, fine #D9D9D9 grid lines, key rows in bold or light red tint #FBE9EB fill — never heavy fills elsewhere.
- KPI panels: oversized red numeral + one-line caption, arranged on white or light-gray square modules.

【Signature Components】
1) Red ❯ title marker + bold red assertion title, top-left: exactly once per body page; the marker is a small solid red ❯, never an outlined or decorative icon.
2) State-coded red step-arrow flow strip: the default way to render any process, pathway, or sequence; red arrows carry active/completed steps, gray arrows carry upcoming steps, black text carries the step names.
3) Square-corner card modules: 1px #D9D9D9 border or light-gray #F5F5F5 fill, red mini-heading + near-black body text; strictly 0 corner radius; 3–6 cards per grid, aligned to one baseline.
4) Oversized red KPI numeral + one-line caption: the core of every data page; 1–3 numerals per page (e.g. 37.3%, 7.6%), each with unit and basis.
5) Red-header comparison table: for product-vs-legacy, product-vs-competitor-class, and config-option comparisons.
6) Software UI screenshot block: screenshot at left 55–60% on white with a fine #D9D9D9 border, paired with a right-side explanation column (red mini-heading + 2–4 short points); the standard way to present product features.
7) Bottom takeaway bar: light-gray strip spanning the body width, opening with a small red ❯, carrying the one-line conclusion.
8) Fine-line architecture diagram: layered boxes with hairline connectors, red reserved for the core/differentiating layer.
9) Fixed brand logo lockup: `assets/mindray-lockup-wide.png` at the bottom-right of every body page, on white or light-gray grounds only; copy the asset into the deck's `media/` directory rather than hot-linking.

【Premium Detailing · 高级感纪律】Distilled from the Med-Tech Precision visual system; these rules supply the premium finish without adding decoration.
1) Two-tier neutral grounds: pure white #FFFFFF surface plus ultra-light neutral #F5F7F8 detail panels (one step lighter than the #F5F5F5 zoning gray) — layering comes from these two quiet tiers, never from shadows or textures.
2) Hairline discipline: every separator, table grid, module border, and connector is a 1px (≤0.75pt) hairline in #D9E0E5 or #D9D9D9; nothing in the deck uses a stroke heavier than 2pt except the cover rule.
3) Spacing rhythm: all margins and gaps follow an 8pt-multiple rhythm (8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64pt); safe page margins are a generous 48–64pt; aligned modules share one baseline and one gap value.
4) Numeric precision: parameters, model numbers, units, and timestamps use tabular figures (mono-style alignment); a figure always carries its unit; never style whole paragraphs as code.
5) Optional dot-grid canvas: architecture/connection diagrams may sit on a low-opacity dot grid (dots ≈1px, rgba(148,163,184,0.28), 24px pitch) laid locally beneath the diagram only — never page-wide, never under text blocks.
6) Quiet hierarchy: type contrast carries the structure (bold red assertion title → near-black section heading → gray caption); no container may exist only to look designed — every box, line, and panel must carry information.
7) Static completeness: every page must read as finished with zero animation; premium feel comes from precision and restraint, not effects.

【Prohibited】No multicolor palettes — no green, blue, orange, purple, or cyan anywhere, including charts, icons, and status marks (positive/negative states use brand red vs. deep maroon or near-black, with text annotation); no rounded corners, pill buttons, or cartoon/hand-drawn elements; no gradients, shadows, glows, 3D effects, or glass textures; no decorative photography or illustration — product photos and software UI screenshots are first-class evidence blocks with white background and a fine border, never a full-page collage; no emoji; no full-red body pages (red dominance is reserved for the cover and closing); do not spread light-gray panels across more than half the body area; never redraw, recolor, or distort the brand logo, and never place text or chart elements over it.

【Density Baseline】Body evidence pages typically carry 2–4 independent modules; card grids 3–6 cards; flows 4–8 nodes; KPI pages 1–3 oversized numerals plus at most one supporting chart; comparison tables 4–8 rows; screenshot pages carry 2–4 explanation points. Explanation blocks are 2–5 lines each; lists 2–4 items per block. Whitespace is concentrated between columns, outside diagrams, and at zone boundaries; leave no solid blank area in the body content region — organize density through zones, fine lines, and square modules rather than decoration.
